Two releases are announced as open-weight, but neither has a public weight repository yet. They are watchlist entries, not catalogue models.

- **[Hy4](https://hy.tencent.ai/research/hy4)** — Tencent released Hy4 on 4 November 2026 as a hosted model. Tencent says it plans to publish the weights and system card. As of 17 November, the Hugging Face API returns no public repository for `tencent/Hy4`.
- **[Hy4 Flash](https://hy.tencent.ai/research/hy4-flash)** — Tencent released Hy4 Flash on 16 November 2026. Tencent says it plans to release it as an open-weight model. No public repository exists yet.
- **[Smaller Qwen4 models](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen4-max)** — Alibaba released Qwen4-Max on 16 November 2026 as a closed, API-only model. Alibaba says smaller Qwen4 models will follow with open weights. None are published yet.

A promise is not a release. The catalogue records a model only after the weights are downloadable and the licence is visible. When that changes, the model gets a record and a Journal entry here.
