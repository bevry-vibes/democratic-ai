Z.ai released **[GLM-5.4](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4)** on 23 November. It is the first flagship built on the lab's Loop Transformer, which runs a shared block of layers for several iterations, and Z.ai reports intelligence comparable to 2T–3T parameter models.

The release is not open yet:

- The API is "coming soon", after a safety evaluation.
- The blog says the weights will be "open-sourced responsibly after completion".

A promise is not a release. GLM-5.4 stays off the catalogue until the weights are downloadable and the licence is visible; the same rule the catalogue applied to Hy4 and the smaller Qwen4 models. `agent-detect` records the rule with openness and licence unset, which is the honest state: not closed, because open weights are announced.

When the weights land, the model gets a record and a Journal entry here.
