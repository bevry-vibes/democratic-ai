The 23 November sweep covered every source — Hugging Face, the blog, the six sitemaps, arXiv, GitHub, and 919 posts from the 50 accounts on the X list. Twenty-four records are new, including older models that re-entered as their metadata moved.

## New labs on the watchlist

- **[Supersonic Labs](https://supersoniclabs.ai/)** publishes decision models under Apache-2.0: [Julia 1](/models/julia-1/), [Julia 1.5](/models/julia-1-5/), [Julia 2](/models/julia-2/), [Julia 2 Flash](/models/julia-2-flash/), and [Julia 2 Nano](/models/julia-2-nano/), the last at 15 MB for microcontrollers. They take a state and a typed question and return a decision, which is a different job from chat; the catalogue records them as classification models.
- **[FluidVoice Labs](https://www.fluidvoice.com/)** is the model arm of FluidVoice. [Fluid Speech Pro](/models/fluid-speech-pro/) is a 0.6B speech-to-text model for noisy rooms, CC-BY-4.0, with GGUF, MLX, ONNX, and CoreML builds attached.
- **[Meta Models](/models/muse-spark-open-816b-a42b/)** is Meta's open-weight org. **Muse Spark Open** is an 816B model with 42B active parameters and an NVFP4 build; [Muse Glimmer 30B](/models/muse-glimmer-30b/) is the smaller Apache-2.0 model with an assistant tune.

## Open weights from newer labs

- **[Rumble 2 Flash](/models/rumble-2-flash/)** — Reson8's 0.5B speech-recognition model for 40+ languages, Apache-2.0.
- **[DaisyRose 27B](/models/daisyrose-27b/)** — RadixArk's multimodal model for visual analysis, planning, and tool use, built on a Qwen base; Apache-2.0, with FP8, NVFP4, and GGUF builds.
- **[Ripple 65B Preview](/models/ripple-65b-preview/)** — Ricursive AI's dense 65B model with a 1M-token context, pretrained on 12 trillion tokens and tuned for agentic coding; Apache-2.0.
- **[Motif 3](/models/motif-3/)** — Motif Technologies' MIT-licensed reasoning model.

## Ant Ling's medical family

**[LingSante 3.1](/models/lingsante-3-1-flash/)** is InclusionAI's medical line, MIT-licensed: the flash model (560B total / 25B active, with an [MXFP4 build](/models/lingsante-3-1-flash-mxfp4/)), [LingSante 3.1 Air VL](/models/lingsante-3-1-air-vl/) at 124B / 5.5B active, and [Pulse 1.0](/models/lingsante-pulse-1-0/), a physiological-signal encoder.

## Also

- **[Inkling 2 Mini](/models/inkling-2-mini/)** — Thinking Machines' Apache-2.0 looped transformer, served on OpenRouter since 21 November.
- **Muse Glimmer 30B Assistant**, and the FlashX GLM 5.3 spellings, joined the entities their providers serve.

## The window moved again

Nine records left as the 90-day window advanced past 20 August: ArmorOCR, SingGuard 2B, the three Ornith 1.5 builds, the LFM2.5 1.2B base, ORZE ASR, and the two Qwen3-ASR conversions. Their repositories remain; the window is the catalogue's scope.

The catalogue now holds 271 records.
