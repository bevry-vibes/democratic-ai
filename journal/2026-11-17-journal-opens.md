The Journal is the dated record for Democratic AI. It covers three kinds of change:

- **Site updates** — new pages, feeds, and corrections.
- **Catalogue changes** — models added, folded, re-licensed, or removed.
- **Watchlist** — models whose weights are promised but not yet released. They are not catalogue records until the weights exist.

The Journal has its own feed at [journal/feed.xml](/journal/feed.xml). The model catalogue has a second feed at [models/feed.xml](/models/feed.xml), ordered by release date, newest first.

The journal is append-only Markdown in the repository. The feeds are generated from the same entries and from the current index, so a reader can follow either the editorial record or the catalogue itself.
