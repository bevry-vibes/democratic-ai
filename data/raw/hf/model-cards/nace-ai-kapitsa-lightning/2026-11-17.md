# Kapitsa Lightning

Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B (NVFP4, 22.9 GB) running locally on Apple Silicon through Kapitsa. Fast replies on 32 GB Macs and up.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model lightning
```

## Speed

| Mac | Prefill (tok/s, 4K prompt) | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| M5 Max 128 GB | 5,738 | 287 |
| M5 Pro 48 GB | 3,141 | 159 |
| M4 Pro 48 GB | 945 | 112 |
| M3 Pro 36 GB | 788 | 75 |

## Architecture

Hybrid Mamba-2 MoE, 30B total parameters, 3B active per token, NVFP4 quantization.
