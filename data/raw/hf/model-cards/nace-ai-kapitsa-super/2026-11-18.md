# Kapitsa Super

Nemotron 3.5 Super VL 120B-A12B (NVFP4, 76.4 GB) running locally on Apple Silicon through Kapitsa. A vision model for 96 GB+ Macs.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model super
```

## Speed

| Mac | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: |
| M5 Max 128 GB | 87 |

## Architecture

Hybrid Mamba-2 MoE, 120B total parameters, 12B active per token, NVFP4 quantization. Supports text and image inputs.
