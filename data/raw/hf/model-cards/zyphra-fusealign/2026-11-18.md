# FuseAlign multilingual (noisy student, step 60,000)

Word-level forced aligner: given a recording and its transcript, it predicts each word's start and end on a 1 ms
grid plus a presence probability. A reproduction of FuseAlign ([Vaidya et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33650))
by Zyphra: one multilingual noisy student, trained 60,000 steps from the multilingual model's CJK fine-tune on about
25,000 h of audio in 73 languages, mostly labelled on clean audio by a teacher per language and learnt from augmented
audio.

Code, usage, long-form alignment and the C++/ggml CLI:
[github.com/Zyphra/fusealign-multilingual](https://github.com/Zyphra/fusealign-multilingual).

## Usage

```python
import soundfile as sf
import torch

aligner = torch.hub.load("Zyphra/fusealign-multilingual", "fusealign", trust_repo=True, device="cuda")
wav, sr = sf.read("speech.wav", dtype="float32")
words = aligner.align(torch.from_numpy(wav), sr, "the transcript of the recording")
# [WordAlignment(word='the', start=..., end=..., presence=..., placed=True), ...]
```

## Evaluation

% of word boundaries off by more than 50 ms (the paper's asymmetric error), against
[Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B) (qwen-asr 0.0.6, bf16, whole clips)
on the same clips, transcripts and references (FuseAlign: the PyTorch package, CUDA, one clip per forward):

| Corpus | FuseAlign | Qwen3-FA |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| TIMIT | 7.92 | 20.17 |
| Buckeye | 7.23 | 24.60 |
| AMI-silver | 34.22 | 58.22 |
| TIMIT-long | 8.21 | 20.04 |
| BZNSYP Mandarin (gold) | 15.44 | 28.04 |
| Seoul Corpus Korean (gold) | 22.43 | 33.58 |
| FLEURS Mandarin | 17.97 | 11.06 |
| FLEURS Japanese | 13.34 | 8.38 |
| FLEURS Korean | 7.80 | 11.53 |
| FLEURS Spanish | 15.43 | 11.71 |
| FLEURS Portuguese | 9.20 | 10.24 |
| FLEURS French | 13.34 | 12.35 |
| FLEURS German | 13.99 | 13.91 |
| FLEURS Russian | 10.60 | 11.18 |

FLEURS references are Montreal Forced Aligner output, not hand labels; BZNSYP's and the Seoul Corpus's are hand-labelled (Seoul: interviewee turns of 40 speakers' spontaneous interviews, at most 30 s).
