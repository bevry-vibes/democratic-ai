# Kapitsa 4B

Qwen3.5 4B (4-bit, 4.6 GB) running locally on Apple Silicon through Kapitsa.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model 4b
```

## Speed

| Mac | Prefill (tok/s) | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| M4 Pro 48 GB | 1,930 | 201 |
| M3 Pro 36 GB | 703 | 120 |
| M1 Pro 16 GB | 401 | 97 |

## Architecture

Dense transformer, 4B parameters, 4-bit quantization.
