# Kapitsa Clover

Clover 35B-A3B (4-bit, 19.5 GB) running locally on Apple Silicon through Kapitsa. DiffuSpace's block-diffusion MoE tuned from Qwen3.6-35B-A3B.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model clover
```

## Speed

| Mac | Prefill (tok/s) | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| M5 Max 128 GB | 5,981 | 304 |

## Architecture

Block-diffusion MoE, 35B total parameters, 3B active per token, 4-bit quantization.
