# Tinfield 1

Tinfield 1 is an agentic model for terminal work and long-horizon software engineering.

It scores **33.0 on Terminal-Bench 4.0**, ahead of Claude Opus 4.8 at 23.6 and Claude Sonnet 5 at 12.4,
and **62 on DeepSWE v1.1**, ahead of Qwen3.8 Max, DeepSeek V4 Flash and Opus 4.8 again. On the
Terminal-Bench board it is the second open-weight model, on a leaderboard otherwise made up entirely of
frontier closed models.

It does that on **6.6B active parameters per token** out of 177B total, and the quantized builds run on a
single 64 GB machine.

Built on [Qwen3.8-Flash-Next](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next). 256k context, BF16 weights.
