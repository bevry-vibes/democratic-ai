# Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2 for Kapitsa

The draft model that [Kapitsa](https://github.com/nace-ai/kapitsa) uses to speed up Qwen3.8 27B on a Mac. It starts from [incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2](https://huggingface.co/incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2) and makes the 27B decode about 6-9% faster on an M5 Pro. Replies are the same as without a drafter, except where the 27B's top two tokens are essentially tied.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model 27b
```

## Speed

M5 Pro 48 GB, Qwen3.8 27B 4-bit, 36 prompts (chat, code, math), greedy, 512 tokens, 3 interleaved rounds.

| Drafter | Tokens per check | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| incoai/Qwen3.8-27B-DFlash2 | 5.04 | 71.7 |
| this drafter | 5.36 | 78.1 |

78.1 is the mean of the two rounds where both drafters ran warm. Counting the first round, where this drafter ran cold, the mean is 75.8 tok/s.

## What changed from incoai's drafter

Same architecture and tensors. The config's `dflash_config.block_size` is 16 (it was 8), because Kapitsa drafts 16 tokens at a time for one conversation. We fine-tuned it for one epoch on 18,000 replies that Qwen3.8 27B (4-bit) wrote at temperature 0, with the DFlash2 training objective at block 16.
