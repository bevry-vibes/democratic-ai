# Kapitsa 9B

Qwen3.5 9B (4-bit, 9.0 GB) running locally on Apple Silicon through Kapitsa.

```bash
kapitsa serve --model 9b
```

## Speed

| Mac | Prefill (tok/s) | Decode (tok/s) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| M3 Pro 36 GB | 381 | 77 |
| M1 Pro 16 GB | 217 | 57 |

## Architecture

Dense transformer, 9B parameters, 4-bit quantization.
